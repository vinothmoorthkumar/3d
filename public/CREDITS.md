## Page content and media — `public/home/`

All text, images, icons and the video on this page come from the UN Careers home page in English and French, https://careers.un.org/home?language=en and https://careers.un.org/home?language=fr (including its "Roster Exercises", "Join Us!", "Consultancies" and "Internships" dialogs), © United Nations. Images were resized and converted to WebP; the 47 MB workforce video streams from careers.un.org and is not bundled. Spelling corrections: "Headquaters" → "Headquarters" (EN), "la crime" → "le crime" and "Type de recruitment" → "Type de recrutement" (FR).

- Staff in the Spotlight graphics link to the original stories on https://unite.un.org/en/WomenInTech-UN (UN Office of Information and Communications Technology).
- Job openings, the duty-station list, announcements and news headlines are loaded live from the UN Careers API.
